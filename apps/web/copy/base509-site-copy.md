## 5. Legal & policy pages (Policies index, Privacy, Terms, + the rest)

**⛔ Boundary — do NOT voice-pass these.** Same rule as the PetAppro site: policy bodies (Terms, Privacy, Cancellation, Refund, Account Ownership, Sub-processors, DPA, Accessibility, Security) are **attorney-drafted legal text**, not marketing copy. The pages already state this ("An attorney drafts and reviews all policy text before publishing") — keep that as the standing behavior, and keep every "Draft / Placeholder — not yet drafted" status label until counsel delivers.

**Framing copy that's fine as-is (keep plain):**
- Policies index intro — good, clear, trust-building: *"These policies govern every Base509 product. Each one is versioned: when a policy changes materially, we publish a new version with a new effective date and notify the people it affects — old versions stay archived here."*
- Privacy placeholder helper + "Questions in the meantime? Contact support@base509.com." — keep.
- The policy-card one-line descriptions — keep.

**⚠️ Flags for Danny / counsel (consistency, not legal advice):**
1. **Terms of Service title mismatch.** On the Base509 site, the ToS page H1 reads **"PetAppro Terms of Service,"** but it sits under Base509 branding and the index card frames it as *"the agreement between Base509 LLC and everyone who uses our software."* Decide which it is and make the title match — this looks like content copied from the PetAppro site without retitling.
2. **Two full policy sets exist** — one on the Base509 site, one on the PetAppro site — with the same policy names and placeholders. Decide the relationship before counsel drafts: are these **company-level canonical policies** (Base509 hosts, PetAppro links to them) or **per-product** copies? Duplicated legal text tends to drift out of sync. Worth settling now so the attorney drafts once, not twice.
3. **Support email consistency** — both sites use `support@base509.com`. Good, keep it consistent.

**Change notes:** no copy changes — this section is a boundary marker + the title/duplication flags above.
