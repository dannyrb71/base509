## 1. Getting set up

**How do I get started?**
Create your account on the web, add the services you offer with your prices and hours, then invite your clients with a link or a QR code. Most providers are set up in an afternoon. *(We're pre-launch right now — join the waitlist and you'll be first in.)*

**How do my clients find me?**
They don't have to — you invite them. Send a link or QR code and they land in your app, under your name. There's no public directory, and we don't drop you into a marketplace alongside everyone else.

**Will you contact or market to my clients?**
No. We don't use your client list to market PetAppro to them, and we don't put another provider in front of them. They're your clients.

**Do you check or approve providers?**
No. We make the software — we don't vet, screen, certify, or rate providers, and using PetAppro isn't a credential. Licensing, insurance, and how you run your business are your decisions to make.

**Do I need a website?**
No. PetAppro is where your clients book, pay, and follow along.

**What services can I offer?**
Boarding, daycare, and dog walking at launch — each with your own prices, hours, capacity, and rules. More service types are on the way. `[TBD: timing]`

**Can I try it before paying?**
Yes. Starter is free for up to 5 clients, and paid plans include a free trial. `[TBD: trial length]`

## 2. Bookings & scheduling

**Do I have to approve every booking?**
Only if you want to. Approve each request, or let your regulars auto-book — you decide per service.

**How do I keep from getting double-booked?**
Set capacity per service — how many dogs you can board at once, how many join a walk — and PetAppro holds the line. Overnight services block each other; walks can run alongside them.

**Can I block off days?**
Yes. Block vacation days, full days, or specific times, and they stop being bookable.

**Can I require a meet-and-greet first?**
Yes, and you choose which services need one. Require it for boarding but not for walks, if that's how you work.

**How do holiday rates work?**
You set them. Holiday weekends cover Friday through Monday automatically, and you can add extended-care and puppy rates. You set the dollar amounts — we don't take a percentage of anything.

**What happens if a client cancels?**
Your cancellation policy applies. You set the window and any fee, and your clients see it before they book.

**Can I offer a flat rate for long stays?**
Yes. Agree the rate with your client the way you normally would, then apply a **price override** on the booking and choose **"Long-stay flat rate"** as the reason. Your client sees the agreed price before they pay, and it's recorded separately in your reports so your discount numbers stay meaningful.

**Is walk tracking available?**
Yes — opt-in GPS tracking for walks, with check-in and check-out coordinates and a route that lands on the client's report card. Available on Crew and up.

## 3. Payments

**How do my clients pay me?**
Through Stripe, directly into your own account. The money goes to you, not through us — we take no cut of your bookings.

**Do I need a Stripe account? How do I set one up?**
Yes, for in-app payments. You can connect an existing Stripe account during setup, or create one first at **[dashboard.stripe.com/register](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register)** — Stripe walks you through signup and verification. Your Stripe account stays yours.

**Are there payment processing fees?**
Stripe charges its standard processing fee on each transaction, and that goes to **Stripe, not to us** — we take no percentage of your bookings. Current rates are on [Stripe's pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing).

**Do my clients pay a service fee?**
No. Your price is the only price — no booking fee or service charge added on their end. What you set is what they pay, and you both see the same total.

**Can I take cash, check, or another payment app?**
Yes. Track payments manually on any plan, and choose which payment types you accept.

**When do I get paid?**
Stripe pays out to your bank on your Stripe payout schedule — we don't hold your money at any point. Payout timing is set by Stripe.

**Can clients leave a tip?**
Yes — tipping is built into in-app payments, and tips go to you. If you take payment outside the app, your clients tip you directly.

## 4. Billing & your subscription

**What does PetAppro cost?**
Plans are a flat monthly price based on team size, from Starter (free, up to 5 clients) through Team. Go annual and a month's on us — see [Pricing](/pricing).

**Do you charge per staff member?**
No per-seat metering. One flat price per tier, and you know the price before you grow into the next one.

**Is there a free trial on paid plans?**
Yes. `[TBD: length — confirmed before launch]`

**How do I cancel?**
In your account settings on the web — one click, self-serve. No phone call, no email, no retention runaround. You keep access through the end of the term you've paid for.

**Where do I manage my plan and billing?**
On the web, in your account. That's where you subscribe, switch plans, and see billing history.

**What happens when I reach Starter's 5-client limit?**
We'll let you know, and you can move up whenever you're ready. Your clients, pets, and history come with you.

**What happens to my data if I leave?**
It's yours. Export your records anytime to a spreadsheet or your accounting software. If you close your account, we walk you through it — and your clients get notice and time to download their own records.
