## Features 1–9

**1. Clear your day as it happens**
Check off a drop-off or pick-up and that pet's card moves out of your way — so you're always looking at what's next, not everything at once.

**2. Every detail, right where you need it**
Emergencies don't give notice. Contacts, care instructions, medications, and each pet's needs are a tap away — ready the moment you need them, no scrambling.

**3. Private notes, just for you**
Jot the things worth remembering — a dog's quirks, a client who tends to run late. Kept private, so nothing slips through the cracks.

**4. Watch your business grow**
A reporting dashboard shows your busiest days and hours, how you're trending, and when you can finally take some time for yourself.

**5. Share the care**
Clients can share a pet's profile with a partner, so the whole household can manage their furry family together.

**6. Make it your brand**
Pick a theme you love, for you and your clients, so the app feels like yours to the people who trust you. New themes added over time.

**7. Your data, your way**
Export anytime to your spreadsheet app or accounting software. It's your business — and your data.

**8. You set how it works**
Require a meet-and-greet on some services and not others. Skip in-app payments if you'd rather. Choose the payment types that fit how you already run.

**9. Room to grow**
Bringing on help? Your plan scales with you — add permission-based roles in a few taps, so the right people see the right things.
