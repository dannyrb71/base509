This draft exercises every layout block an author can use, so George can copy
its patterns when writing real content. It never ships: `published: false`
keeps it out of the index, the sitemap, and static params — visiting its URL
returns a 404.

## Plain markdown works everywhere

Regular paragraphs, **bold**, _italic_, [links](https://petappro.com), and
lists render through the standard `.prose` styles:

- Boarding, daycare, and walking each get their own capacity rules.
- Holiday weekends cover Friday through Monday automatically.
- Your clients see your prices — PetAppro never takes a percentage.
