# Accessibility Statement

## OUR COMMITMENT

We want every pet-care provider and every pet owner to be able to use PetAppro — including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology. This isn't a compliance checkbox for us. Excluding people isn't who we are.

## WHAT WE'RE BUILDING TOWARD

We are building PetAppro — the app, the provider portal, and our websites — to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We say "building toward" deliberately: we won't claim we conform to a standard until we've actually tested against it.

In practice, that means we design and build for:

- Text and interface contrast that meets AA minimums — in every theme, in both light and dark modes
- Full keyboard reachability and operability, with a visible focus indicator
- Meaningful labels for screen readers, and alternative text for images
- Touch targets large enough to hit comfortably
- Never relying on color alone to convey meaning
- Text that can be resized without breaking the layout

## HOW WE CHECK IT

Our approach as we build: automated accessibility checks in our build pipeline, contrast verified across every theme × light/dark as part of the design system, and manual keyboard + screen-reader passes on the core flows. Once we've completed a full evaluation (and any third-party audit), we'll say so here and name the date.

## WHERE WE KNOW WE FALL SHORT

As we test, we'll list known gaps here honestly — a real accessibility statement names what doesn't work yet. We'd rather tell you than pretend everything works. (This section is updated as testing surfaces issues.)

## TELL US WHEN WE GET IT WRONG

If you hit a barrier in PetAppro, please tell us — we'd rather hear it from you than not know.

support@base509.com — tell us what you were trying to do, where, and what assistive technology you use, if any. We'll respond and tell you what we're doing about it. If a barrier stops you from doing something, we'll find another way to help you get it done while we fix the underlying issue.
