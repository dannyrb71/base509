## 4. What you pay, and who you pay

**You do not pay PetAppro for using PetAppro.** Providers pay us for the software. You pay **your provider**.

### Prices and booking totals

Your Provider sets its prices, optional services, contingent fees, taxes, cancellation terms, and refund policies.

**The price displayed for a booking will include mandatory Provider fees and charges that can be determined at that time**, excluding government-imposed taxes that may lawfully be displayed separately. Optional tips and optional additional services are not included unless you select them.

The booking-review screen will show **the amount currently authorized, when it will be charged, and any disclosed contingent cancellation or no-show charge.**

A booking estimate may change before you submit the booking if you change dates, services, pets, or other pricing inputs. **After submission, the Provider may not increase the authorized booking amount or add a mandatory fee without your separate affirmative agreement** — except for a contingent fee calculated exactly as disclosed and authorized at booking.

> `[⛔ PRODUCT BLOCKER — California all-in pricing (SB 478). If we display a price to a California consumer, it generally **must include mandatory Provider fees** other than government-imposed taxes. **Disclosing a mandatory surcharge only at final checkout is insufficient.** ([CA DOJ](https://oag.ca.gov/hiddenfees)) This constrains **D-043** (off-hours surcharge) and any Provider-configured mandatory fee: a mandatory surcharge must be **in the displayed price**, not revealed at checkout. → README #30.]`

### Saved payment methods and later charges

Stripe handles saved payment credentials. If you choose to save a payment method, the consent screen will identify:
- **the Provider authorized to use it**;
- whether it may be used only while you are actively checking out, or also for authorized **off-session** charges;
- the **types of transactions** for which it may be used;
- the anticipated **timing or frequency**; and
- **how any future amount will be determined.**

**A payment method saved for one Provider does not authorize another Provider to charge it.**

A Provider may charge an authorized saved payment method after checkout **only** for:
- the booking amount and timing you accepted;
- a cancellation or no-show fee **whose exact amount or objective calculation you accepted**;
- an installment or other scheduled amount you specifically accepted; or
- another charge **you separately approve after its amount and purpose are disclosed.**

**An authorization for a Provider's policies is not authorization for an unlimited or unspecified charge.**

**Added services, price increases, and alleged damage charges require separate affirmative approval after the amount is disclosed.** If you do not approve, the Provider may pursue an invoice or other remedy available under its agreement and applicable law, **but may not use PetAppro to make an arbitrary charge against your saved payment method.**

> `[⛔ PRODUCT BLOCKERS — two, and both are money-safety:]`
> 1. **Damage charges and added services CANNOT ride on booking-time consent.** Stripe requires explicit consent for saving a payment method **and** for the specified future/off-session uses, including anticipated timing and how amounts will be determined. ([Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-and-reuse?platform=web&ui=embedded-form)) Cancellation/no-show fees **can** be authorized at booking *if the exact amount or objective calculation is clear.* **Damage = new approval, or the Provider invoices.** This constrains the D-043 surcharge and the timed-service no-show fee designs. → README #3, #31.
> 2. **Saved payment methods must be Provider-scoped — this is tenant isolation with money attached.** Direct charges go to **separate Standard connected accounts**; a method saved for Provider A **must not silently become chargeable by Provider B.** The authorization *and the technical payment object* must be scoped to the identified merchant. → README #32.

### Stripe payments, refunds, and disputes

When PetAppro uses direct charges to a Provider's eligible Stripe account:
- **the Provider is the merchant of record**;
- the payment is made to the Provider;
- **the Provider is identified in checkout, on the receipt, and through the applicable statement descriptor**;
- the Provider is responsible for its services, refunds, and chargebacks; and
- **PetAppro does not receive a commission or application fee.**

**PetAppro may transmit a payment or refund instruction authorized by the Provider, but PetAppro does not decide the merits of a refund request and does not fund Provider refunds from PetAppro's own money.**

**Contact the Provider first** about a refund or billing error.

**Nothing in these Client Terms limits rights you may have under applicable consumer law, payment-network rules, or your agreement with your bank or card issuer.**

PetAppro doesn't adjudicate disputes between you and your provider. We may be able to provide records of what the software recorded — bookings, invoices, timestamps, report cards — where appropriate and permitted.

**This matters when you delete your account** (§9) — see the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

### Information about your pets

Information about an animal can still be personal information when it's linked to you or your household. We treat it accordingly.

### Home-access information

If you provide **entry instructions, lockbox codes, alarm codes, or similar information**, you direct PetAppro to make that information available to the Provider for the requested service.

**Access should be limited to Provider personnel authorized by their role or assignment to perform or administer the applicable service** — not necessarily every person the Provider has authorized generally.

- We treat it as **restricted information** with additional access controls.
- **You can remove or update it at any time**, and should when it's no longer needed.
- **Consider using a temporary or service-specific code, and changing it after the Provider relationship ends.**

## 9. Ending your use of PetAppro

### Leaving a provider

**You can end a relationship with one provider without deleting your account** — your other providers, pets, and history are unaffected.

Leaving a provider doesn't cancel bookings you've already made, or resolve amounts you owe them. It doesn't require your provider to delete records they're entitled to keep.

### Deleting your account

You may initiate deletion **from inside the app or through our web deletion resource.** We may require recent authentication to protect the account.

**Before confirmation, the deletion flow will identify** active services, future bookings, possible cancellation consequences, available exports, and information that may be retained.

**If you select immediate deletion:**
- active PetAppro sessions and sign-in credentials are revoked;
- PetAppro-controlled account information is deleted or deidentified subject to the [Privacy Policy](/policies/privacy);
- **Sign in with Apple tokens are revoked** where applicable;
- **unstarted future bookings are cancelled, with simultaneous notice to you and each affected Provider**;
- **any properly disclosed cancellation consequences are shown before confirmation** and handled under the Provider policy you previously accepted;
- **active services enter a restricted completion or safety-handoff state** only as long as reasonably necessary; and
- Provider relationships are ended or unlinked as described in the deletion flow.

**PetAppro may also offer deletion scheduled after your last active booking, but scheduled deletion will not be the only option.**

**Deleting your account does not** erase every record independently controlled and lawfully retained by a Provider, eliminate an accrued payment obligation, or waive a refund or other right you may have.

### When your provider closes

If your provider closes their PetAppro business, we'll notify you and give you **30 days** of read-only access to download the records available to you from that relationship — your pets' information and care instructions, report cards and photos, booking history, invoices and receipts, and your provider's closure contact information.

**Future bookings are cancelled**, and you and your provider are notified at the same time. **Where you prepaid for a service your provider won't perform, your provider is responsible for a full refund** — see [Provider Terms](/policies/provider-terms) §9.

Your relationships with **other** providers are unaffected.

### When we end access

We may restrict, suspend, or end access if an account violates these Terms, creates a security, safety, animal-welfare, legal, or payment risk, or where we must act to comply with law.

Where circumstances reasonably permit, we'll give notice and a chance to fix the issue. We may act immediately where necessary to prevent harm, protect someone's safety, or comply with law.

**Ending your access does not eliminate amounts you owe a provider, or affect your provider's obligations to you.**
