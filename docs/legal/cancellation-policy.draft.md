## The short version

**Cancelling is as easy as subscribing.** One control, in the same place you signed up. No phone call, no email, no "let me transfer you to retention."

---

## 1. This page is about *your subscription*

Two different cancellations get confused, so let's separate them:

- **This page:** cancelling your **PetAppro subscription** — the plan you pay us for.
- **Not this page:** a **client cancelling a booking** with a provider. That's governed by **the provider's own cancellation policy**, which the provider sets in PetAppro and shows their clients at booking. We're not part of that — [we're software, not the pet-care provider](/policies/terms).

---

## 2. How to cancel

1. Sign in at **app.petappro.com**
2. Go to **Billing**
3. Click **Cancel subscription**

That's it. It takes effect immediately, and we'll confirm by email.

**We will never** make you call us, email us, chat with an agent, or navigate a maze to cancel. If you ever can't find the button, that's a bug — tell us at support@base509.com and we'll fix it *and* cancel you.

---

## 3. What happens when you cancel

- **During a free trial:** cancel before the trial ends and **you are not charged**. Nothing.
- **During a paid term:** you keep full access until the end of the term you've already paid for. We don't cut you off early.
- **At the end of the term:** your subscription stops renewing, and your account moves to a limited free state. You keep access to your own business records — we never lock you out of your own data. `[COUNSEL/PRODUCT: confirm the exact end-state — limited free tier vs. read-only. Intent locked: a provider never loses access to their own business records.]`
- **Your data:** cancelling a subscription is **not** the same as deleting your account. Your data stays until you delete it. See [Privacy](/policies/privacy) §7 and [Account Ownership](/policies/ownership).
- **Refunds:** see the [Refund Policy](/policies/refund).

## 4. Free trials, and what you agree to

Before you start a trial, we tell you — **on the signup screen, before you consent**:

- how long the trial lasts,
- the exact amount you'll be charged when it ends,
- how often you'll be charged after that,
- and that it renews automatically until you cancel.

You have to affirmatively agree to that. **No pre-checked boxes.**

---

## 5. Automatic renewal, and our reminders

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. To make sure that's never a surprise:

- We email a **reminder before your trial converts** to a paid subscription.
- We email a **renewal reminder at least once a year**, whatever your billing period.
- Every reminder contains a direct link to cancel.

## 6. If we offer you something on the way out

If we ever show you an offer while you're cancelling, it will be **one screen, skippable, and it will never stand between you and cancelling.** Your cancellation goes through either way.

## 7. If we cancel

We may suspend or end an account for non-payment or a violation of the [Terms](/policies/terms). Except where immediate action is needed, we'll give notice and a chance to fix it, and we won't hold your data hostage — see [Account Ownership](/policies/ownership).

> **⚠️ CORRECTED 2026-07-16.** An earlier draft asserted CARL applies to us. That was overstated — see below.

**California Automatic Renewal Law (CARL / AB 2863)** — governs contracts entered, amended, or extended on/after **2025-07-01**, and expressly covers **"free-to-pay conversions."** *However:* **CARL likely does NOT cover most Provider subscriptions.** California defines a covered **"consumer"** as an individual acquiring services for **personal, family, or household purposes** (Bus. & Prof. Code §§17601–17602). A sole proprietor buying PetAppro to **operate a pet-care business** probably isn't a covered consumer merely because the business is unincorporated. **Purpose controls — counsel please confirm.**

**We comply anyway**, deliberately: it's the safer national policy, and it's how we've said we operate. Requirements we've written to:
1. **Click-to-cancel in the same medium** (§2).
2. **Clear disclosure + express affirmative consent** before the trial converts (§4).
3. **Annual renewal reminders**, regardless of billing period (§5).
4. **Rules on retention/save offers** during cancellation (§6).

**Federal — corrected:** the FTC's 2024 nationwide "Click-to-Cancel" rule was **vacated** (8th Cir., July 2025), and in **February 2026 the FTC formally restored the older, narrower rule** (91 FR 6507). **Don't cite the vacated rule as binding.** But **ROSCA (15 U.S.C. §8403) independently requires** clear disclosure, express informed consent, and a **simple mechanism to stop recurring internet charges** — that applies to us regardless. General FTC deception prohibitions and state laws also apply.

**Please confirm:**
- CARL's application to business-purpose subscriptions (we're not relying on the exemption either way).
- Reminder timing/content against §5 and the Provider Terms §4 schedule.
- Whether recurring bookings/packages/memberships a **provider** sells to **their** clients trigger CARL for the provider — if so, our template should help them comply.
- **Note:** Housecall Pro (adjacent space) still requires *email* to cancel. We are not following that model.
