## The short version

If PetAppro charged you for something you didn't want or use, email us and we'll make it right. We're not interested in keeping money we didn't earn.

---

## 1. Two kinds of money — only one is ours

- **Your PetAppro subscription** — paid to Base509 LLC. **This page covers that.**
- **A booking payment** — a client paying a provider. That money goes **directly to the provider's own Stripe account; we never touch it and take no cut.** Refunds for a booking are **the provider's** decision under **the provider's** refund policy. Ask your provider. We can't refund money we never received.

---

## 2. Subscription refunds

- **Free trial:** cancel before it ends and you're never charged — nothing to refund.
- **Charged by mistake?** Email us. If you were double-billed, charged after cancelling, or charged for a plan you didn't choose — **we refund it in full.** No argument.
- **Forgot to cancel and just got billed?** Email us within **30 days** and we'll refund the term if you haven't meaningfully used it. We'd rather refund you than have you resent us.
- **Cancelled mid-term:** you keep access through the end of the term you paid for; we don't prorate a refund for the unused part by default. `[COUNSEL/PRODUCT: confirm — some jurisdictions require prorated refunds on annual plans; consider offering it for annual regardless.]`
- **Annual plans:** cancel an annual plan within the first **30 days** and we'll refund the unused portion on request.

## 3. How to ask

Email **support@base509.com** with the account email and roughly what happened. We'll reply within **2 business days**. Refunds go back to the original payment method; your bank takes 5–10 days after we send it.

## 4. Chargebacks

If something's wrong, please just ask us first — it's faster than a chargeback and we'll almost certainly say yes. `[COUNSEL: standard language reserving rights on abusive chargebacks; also note chargebacks on *booking* payments are between the client, the provider, and Stripe — not us.]`
