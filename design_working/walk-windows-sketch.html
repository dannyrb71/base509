Walk Windows

Availability layer for dog walking. Sits in Business Settings as a sibling section to Walking Rate Options — rates say what a walk costs, windows say when walks happen and how many dogs fit. Clients book a window + days, never an exact time.

Client App

What the client sees

The client never picks a clock time. They pick service → days → window → recurrence. Capacity («spots») is the live count for group walks in their zone. GPS tracking pays off the wide window with an “on the way” heads-up.
